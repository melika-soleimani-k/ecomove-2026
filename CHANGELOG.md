# Changelog

All notable changes to EcoMove 2026 are documented here. Dates follow the hackathon period
(June to August 2026).

## 1.1.0 - 2026-08-27

### Changed
- CO₂e figures restated as positive emissions with avoided-vs-driving framing (+2.4 kg eco,
  +3.1 kg shuttle, 1.8 and 1.1 kg avoided); all negative-carbon claims removed
- Transit labeling replaces metro naming, since BMO Field is not served by TTC subway
- Gate states aligned to one band definition (CLEAR under 10, MODERATE 10 to 16, HEAVY 17 and above)
  with three match-clock presets: Quiet, Rising, Peak
- KPI tiles labeled modeled or scenario assumption; SIMULATED FEED disclosure added to the header
- Route timeline restated as an illustrative 12-min pre-gate sequence (transit 6, walk 3, scooter 3)
- Match-day impact restated with mode-shift scenario (~1.2 t, ~$9.4K) and upper-bound case
  (~3.4 t, ~$27.5K) shown separately

### Added
- City selector with planning-level transferability views (MetLife, Lumen Field)
- docs/ folder: methodology, data dictionary, design system, roadmap
- CONTRIBUTING.md with data-integrity ground rules

### Removed
- Unsupported claims (tree-equivalence wording, live-data implication)

## 1.0.0 - 2026-06-24

### Added
- Initial hackathon build: operations dashboard, eco route planner, 7-token design system
- Dependency-free HTML/CSS/SVG prototype deployed to GitHub Pages
- Simulated data layer with documented backend endpoints
